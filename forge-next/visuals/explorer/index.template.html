TAKE YOUR TIME HERE

It is a place to explore.

Find a thread

Walk through follows one actual piece, weight, channel, hidden unit and output. Move at your own pace with Previous and Next; optional autoplay pauses when you interact. The seven chapters are also available. Each scene has its own camera and explanation. Use the game slider to carry another position through the same architecture.

Move through it

Drag to orbit; both axes follow your hand. Scroll or pinch toward the pointer. Right-drag or Shift-drag pans. Click selects without reframing; double-click, Focus selected or F makes it the orbit centre. Back restores the previous selection and camera. In Fly inside, drag to look and use W/A/S/D to move, Q/E down/up and Shift for speed. Escape returns to the camera view you had before flight. Home and Overview fit the current view. Hiding the inspector does not reset the camera.

Go smaller

Click a hidden neuron: inspect its exact sum and clipping. Follow its incoming terms back to pieces, or its outputs forward to W/D/L and move scores. Choose Strongest 12 or All terms for the scene’s connections. Click a piece on the small chessboard to trace its placement lookup. Weight universe exposes every weight of a chosen king bucket as a point.

Know what is exact

The entire deployed binary is embedded. The forward evaluator is checked against 128 saved native-engine readings. Camera layout, animation speed and glow are illustration; the page does not run search or reproduce the engine’s memory-cache implementation.
